Free ICA training

Stop typing. Start thinking out loud.

Most people use ChatGPT like a search bar. Voice Mode is different. In this free training, you will see how to use ChatGPT as a practical thinking partner—to develop ideas, prepare for important conversations and get useful work done while you speak naturally.

What you will learn

  1. 01
    Brainstorm ideas through conversation

    Move beyond one-line prompts and use follow-up questions to develop, challenge and improve your thinking.

  2. 02
    Prepare for talks and important conversations

    Rehearse your message, organise your thoughts and practise answering questions before you step into the real conversation.

  3. 03
    Work through forms and documents

    Talk through difficult questions, clarify what is being asked and shape clear answers without staring at a blank page.

  4. 04
    Translate in real time

    Use natural spoken translation to communicate across languages and keep a conversation moving.

If you have only been typing, this will change your workflow.

Watch the lesson once for the full picture. Then open ChatGPT Voice Mode and practise one of the examples immediately—the value appears when you turn the training into a real conversation.
